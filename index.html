// about physicstools

Made for Teachers,
Built by SimLabs

PhysicsTools is a free, browser-based platform developed by SimLabs to give physics educators professional-grade tools — without the price tag or the software install.

Every tool is designed to the DBE-CAPS standard, so diagrams look exactly like what students see in past papers.

100%
Free to use, always
0

DBE-CAPS Standard

Circuit symbols and notation match the DBE-CAPS standard used in South African physics examinations.

Instant Load

No login, no loading screen. Open a tool and start working in under three seconds.

4× High-Res Export

Diagrams export at 4× resolution — sharp when printed or embedded in a PDF.

Growing Toolkit
